LISTEN AND REPEAT SECTION A dedicated listening–speaking reinforcement module designed to improve accent, clarity, rhythm, tone, and natural spoken English through active imitation. 1. Listen to Audios and Repeat the Same What learners do: • Listen to short audio clips containing clear, well-structured sentences • Repeat each sentence exactly as heard, focusing on pronunciation, rhythm, and sound accuracy • Practice shadow speaking by matching the pace and flow of the original audio • Use slow-mode and fast-mode listening options for stepwise improvement Why it matters: • Strengthens sound recognition and pronunciation • Helps learners absorb natural rhythm and sentence flow • Reduces mother-tongue influence through consistent imitation • Builds the foundation for accent clarity and confident speaking Add  ons : • AI provides instant pronunciation scoring • Option to slow down or break audios into syllables • Daily audio packs for general English, workplace English, and interview English 2. Listen to Dialogues and Speak with Intonation What learners do: • Listen to short conversational dialogues between two speakers • Practice repeating each line with the correct emotional tone, pitch variation, and intonation • Recreate the dialogue as both Speaker A and Speaker B • Learn natural conversational cues such as polite pauses, emphasis, and expressive delivery Why it matters: • Builds conversational naturalness and expression • Helps learners speak with emotion and confidence instead of monotone delivery • Improves fluency when interacting in real conversations • Enhances clarity and listener engagement Add  ons : • AI highlights tone mismatches and suggests corrections • Character-based dialogue packs: workplace, hotel booking, interview desk, group discussion • Dialogue speed-training mode to improve flow 3. Listen to TED Talks and Recreate the Dialogues What learners do: • Listen to selected sections from TED Talks and repeat them line by line • Analyze speaker’s pacing, pauses, storytelling tone, and emphasis • Attempt to recreate the same segment in their own voice • Practice both exact replication and paraphrased recreation Why it matters: • Develops advanced spoken English rhythm and presentation style • Builds confidence in long-form speaking and storytelling • Exposes learners to global accents and high-quality speech patterns • Strengthens voice control and delivery for presentations and interviews Add  ons : • AI extracts key phrases, tone markers, and storytelling elements • Practice mode for recreating 15-second, 30-second, and 1-minute TED segments • Performance analysis on clarity, tone, and expressive delivery 4. Listen to Interview Sentences and Repeat with Accent, Tone, and Intonation What learners do: • Listen to standard HR and technical interview sentences • Repeat them with correct accent, tone, emphasis, and professional speech rhythm • Train to sound confident, calm, and articulate in a business context • Practice typical responses such as introductions, strengths, responsibilities, and project explanations Why it matters: • Teaches learners to sound professional in interviews • Builds confidence, clarity, and vocal control • Helps them adopt workplace-appropriate tone and pacing • Enhances impression-making ability during the actual interview Add  ons : • AI offers accent-neutral English audio samples • Specialized packs: HR tone pack, leadership tone pack, customer-service tone pack • Side-by-side comparison feature where learners hear both voices to identify differences 5. Accent Neutralization and Sound Clarity Practice What learners do:   • Practice repeating short sound drills focused on vowels, consonant clusters, and stress patterns • Work on removing extra sounds and adjusting mother-tongue influence • Repeat accent-neutral phrases used in global workplaces Why it matters: • Helps learners sound clearer and more globally understandable • Removes unclear pronunciation patterns • Improves confidence when speaking to non-native listeners Add  ons : • AI highlights pronunciation breakdown with syllable-level feedback • Daily accent-neutralization micro-drills
