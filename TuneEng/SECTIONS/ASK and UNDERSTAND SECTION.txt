ASK & UNDERSTAND SECTION A guided clarification and comprehension module designed to help learners ask questions, understand concepts deeply, and immediately turn learning into speaking practice. 1. Ask Doubts on Grammar, Tech, English, Personality, Life Skills What learners do: • Ask questions freely about grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, writing, or any English-related concept • Ask doubts on technology, digital tools, software basics, or tech concepts they struggle to understand • Ask questions related to personality development, confidence-building, habits, communication ethics, or workplace behavior • Receive structured, clear explanations tailored to their understanding level Why it matters: • Helps learners build a strong foundation by clarifying confusion immediately • Prevents accumulation of misunderstandings that affect fluency and confidence • Supports holistic development by covering personal, academic, and professional areas • Encourages curiosity and independent learning, building self-directed communicators Add  ons : • AI adapts explanations based on learner’s difficulty level • Option to ask follow-up questions until the concept is fully mastered 2. AI Explains and Converts  Into  Voice Practice What learners do: • Receive a clear and simplified explanation of their question • Get the explanation converted into a speaking model for them to practice aloud • Practice repeating, paraphrasing, or summarizing the explanation using voice • Use AI cues to improve pronunciation, intonation, and clarity while speaking the concept back Why it matters: • Transforms passive learning into active speaking practice • Helps learners internalize complex ideas through repeatable voice drills • Strengthens memory and articulation by speaking instead of only reading • Builds fluency by teaching learners to explain ideas clearly—an essential interview skill Add  ons : • AI provides a short, medium, and long explanation format for flexible practice • Learners can convert any explanation into a self-introduction or interview-style answer format
