COMPANY SPECIFIC SECTION A targeted preparation module designed to help learners practice interview questions tailored to specific companies, roles, tools, and real placement patterns. 1. Role-Based Questions What learners do: • Practice interview questions customized for specific job roles such as software developer, data analyst, UI/UX designer, HR trainee, sales executive, operations associate, or content writer • Answer questions related to responsibilities, required skills, domain knowledge, and role expectations • Connect their experiences, strengths, and projects to role requirements • Practice short, structured, and confident explanations to justify role alignment Why it matters: • Helps learners prepare for the actual type of questions asked for their target job • Strengthens ability to show role–candidate fit • Builds clarity on responsibilities and expectations before the interview • Improves relevance and precision in answers Add  ons : • AI generates a detailed role matrix (skills, responsibilities, questions, expected answers) • Quick practice sets for fresher-level, entry-level, and mid-level job roles 2. Tool-Based Questions What learners do: • Answer questions related to specific tools used by the company: Excel, Power BI, SAP, Figma, Git, SQL tools, Java frameworks, Python libraries, CRM tools, or cloud platforms • Explain real-world use-cases, challenges, troubleshooting steps, and past experiences • Describe how these tools helped them accomplish tasks or solve problems • Practice simplifying technical features when speaking to HR Why it matters: • Companies look for tool familiarity more than deep expertise • Shows technical readiness for the role • Improves the learner’s ability to explain tools confidently and practically • Helps learners sound job-ready and technically aware Add  ons : • AI creates tool-specific flashcards and real-use case questions • Practice mode where learners explain a tool in 30, 60, or 90 seconds 3. Based on Previously Asked Questions in Real Placements What learners do: • Practice real interview questions asked in actual campus placements across companies • Review common patterns, difficulty levels, and frequently repeated questions • Build familiarity with company expectations, tone, and style • Prepare answers aligned with actual hiring patterns instead of generic questions Why it matters: • Gives learners a major advantage by learning from real past interviews • Reduces unpredictability • Helps them anticipate follow-up questions • Builds confidence through familiarity Add  ons : • AI offers company-wise question banks: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Zoho, Accenture, HCL, Deloitte, Cognizant, L&T, Tech Mahindra • Company difficulty-level filters: basic, moderate, advanced DYNAMICS A real-time, challenge-based practice system where learners train using interactive company-oriented simulations. 1. Pick Company Challenge What learners do: • Select a target company (e.g., Infosys, TCS, Deloitte, Zoho) • Complete a timed set of questions based on that company’s interview style • Respond to a mix of HR, technical HR, behavioral, and situational questions • Receive performance insights and targeted improvement points Why it matters: • Mimics the real company interview environment • Builds company-specific confidence • Helps learners adapt to varying interview styles and expectations • Strengthens preparedness through focused practice Add  ons : • Multi-round company simulation (HR → Technical HR → Final Round) • AI-generated scorecard with company match percentage 2. Answer Domain-Specific Interview Questions What learners do: • Practice answering questions from their specific domain: IT, finance, analytics, HR, marketing, operations, design • Explain domain concepts, tools, workflows, and common challenges • Demonstrate understanding of industry standards • Respond to scenario-based domain questions Why it matters: • Domain knowledge often decides final selection • Builds subject-matter credibility • Helps learners articulate professional knowledge clearly • Bridges academic learning with real job expectations Add  ons : • Domain challenge sets for 20+ fields • Role-based domain scoring and next-step suggestions 3. Pick Tool Challenge What learners do: • Choose a tool (Excel, SQL, Figma, Git, Canva, Power BI, Python libraries) • Answer instant tool-related questions • Explain features, usage, troubleshooting, and examples • Respond to scenario questions involving real tool application Why it matters: • Builds readiness for practical tool discussions in interviews • Helps learners connect tool knowledge with real job tasks • Shows interviewers confidence in technical execution Add  ons : • Instant “Explain this tool in 45 seconds” mode •  multi-scenario  tool challenge (task breakdown, error handling, optimization ideas)
