TuneEng  AI – Trainer Section A fully integrated, institution-ready ecosystem designed to empower verbal trainers to manage classrooms, create assessments, host interactive speaking activities, and evaluate learners using multi-modal AI—while preserving complete trainer control over rubrics, scoring, and feedback. 1. Overview The Trainer Section enables verbal trainers across institutions to seamlessly create, assign, and evaluate quizzes, GDs, debates, JAMs, discussions, written tests, and situational tasks. It merges classroom management, assessment tools, AI-assisted scoring, and analytics into a single trainer-powered environment. Trainers retain full authority over rubrics, scoring, visibility settings, and feedback, while AI accelerates grading, offers explainable insights, corrects grammar, analyzes audio/video responses, and provides adaptive recommendations. 2. Key Features A. Classroom Management Create and manage multiple classrooms (Google Classroom style). Enroll students via invitation links, batch uploads, or institution roster sync. Assign pre-tests, post-tests, weekly/bi-weekly tasks, challenges, and speaking activities. Track classroom-level and student-level performance over time. Manage multiple batches with full visibility and activity logs. Auto-generate class schedules and sync them with calendar integrations. B. Quiz, Test & Assignment Creation Create written and verbal tests: MCQs, short answers, long answers, audio/video responses, cloze tests, reading comprehension, sentence correction, paraphrasing, JAM prompts. Type questions manually or upload in bulk; AI formats them automatically. AI Audio Conversion: Typed questions instantly converted into natural-speech audio prompts. AI Video Conversion: Optional animated presenter reading questions for video-based tests. Trainer-provided model answers enhance precision of AI scoring. Grammar correction layer for student submissions (text, audio-transcripts). Multi-Modal Evaluation: Tone, pitch, pace, intonation Accent, articulation, clarity Delivery, confidence, verbal structure For video: body language, eye contact, posture Google Forms Integration: One-click export from  TuneEng  to Google Forms One-click import of responses for AI scoring Deadline, reminders, attempt limits, late policies, and auto-locking controls. C. Situational Teaching & Scenario Evaluation Add real-world professional and academic scenarios (technical and non-technical). AI provides explainable feedback such as: “This is a good interpretation, but here is a clearer way to frame it.” Trainers observe how students handle situational stress and decision-making. Video-based role-play tasks evaluated for clarity, confidence, and problem-solving. Adaptive follow-up questions generated automatically. D. Speaking Activities: GD, Debate, Discussions, JAM (Trainer-Controlled) Conduct GDs, debates, discussions, panel discussions, and JAM sessions with AI moderation. AI handles: Turn-taking Timekeeping Prompting Flagging interruptions, weak arguments, rule violations Transcription + scoring Trainers retain override controls for: Scoring Reordering speakers Adding/removing participants Injecting hints or correction notes Pausing/resuming sessions Assigning penalty or bonus points Rubrics tailor-made for each format: GD: participation balance, content depth, teamwork, clarity Debate: argument strength, rebuttals, evidence, persuasiveness Discussions: coherence, logic, interaction quality JAM: fluency, no hesitation/repetition/deviation E. Analytics & Reporting Student-level dashboards: Skill proficiency Grammar accuracy Fluency metrics Tone and intonation trends Response time analysis Classroom-level analytics: Completion rates Difficulty heatmaps Top performers and struggling learners Compare pre-test and post-test performance. AI-generated insights: strengths, weaknesses, recommended improvement paths. Leaderboards for class-wide and global  TuneEng  challenges. Exportable reports for academic and placement documentation. F. Community & Engagement Assign students to debates, GD rooms, discussion circles. AI moderates and scores peer interactions. Peer review with AI auditing ensures fairness. Classroom announcements and activity threads for updates. 3. Trainer Workflows A. Classroom Setup Create classroom → Add students → Auto-generate welcome kit → Assign diagnostic pre-test. B. Quiz & Test Creation Type questions → AI formats and converts into audio/video → Add model answers → Set rubrics → Assign to classrooms → Schedule with deadlines/reminders. C. Student Evaluation Students respond via text, audio, or video. AI evaluates grammar, structure, content depth, tone, pacing, clarity, and delivery. Trainers verify and override AI scoring when needed. AI generates explainable feedback for each mistake or partial correctness. D. Progress Tracking & Reporting Monitor dashboards → Identify weak areas → Assign targeted remedial tasks. Compare cohorts, batches, and timelines. Share reports with students, parents, or placement cells. E. Community & Peer Engagement Organize GD/Debate/JAM → AI moderates → Scores generated → Trainer reviews → Feedback shared. 4. AI Components for Trainer Section A. Audio & Video Analysis Prosody: tone, pitch, stress, intonation Speech clarity and articulation Accent analysis with neutralization suggestions Video posture & body language insights B. Grammar Correction & Answer Refinement Auto-correct text responses Convert audio/video to accurate transcripts Highlight grammar, tense, structure, vocabulary mistakes Provide better phrasings and alternative answers C. Situation-Based Evaluation Evaluates decision-making, clarity, empathy, and professional reasoning Provides step-by-step feedback describing the expected standard 5.
