INSTITUTION SECTION Overview The Institution Section enables colleges, universities, schools, and corporate training divisions to manage English fluency development at scale. Administrators gain complete visibility over trainers, students, classrooms, subscriptions, and performance analytics. The module aligns institution goals with AI-powered insights, ensuring measurable progress, standardized training quality, and seamless coordination across departments and campuses.
